Category Theory for Collective Intelligence

Exploring how category theory can illuminate deep structural patterns in collective intelligence systems, focusing on information flows and emergent behaviors.

Project Overview

This project explores how category theory can illuminate the deep structural patterns in collective intelligence systems, particularly focusing on how different components interact and combine to create emergent behaviors. The research aims to develop formal mathematical frameworks that capture how information flows and transforms across different scales of collective systems, from scientific communities to markets.

The insights could help design systems that naturally preserve beneficial properties as they scale and evolve.

Approach & Methodology

The team embodies a Platonic approach to mathematics, seeking to uncover fundamental structures that exist across different manifestations of collective intelligence. Their methodology draws from the tradition of pure mathematics - starting with abstract patterns and gradually connecting them to concrete implementations.

Morphological Computing in Social Systems

Investigating how biological systems maintain coherent behavior across multiple scales without central control.

Project Overview

This research investigates how biological systems maintain coherent behavior across multiple scales without central control, drawing heavily on Michael Levin's work on morphological computation. The project examines how principles from biological collective intelligence - from embryonic development to ant colonies - could inform the design of more robust artificial systems.

The goal is to understand and replicate nature's solutions to key AI safety challenges like maintaining goal-directedness without rigid hierarchies.

Approach & Methodology

This group follows a naturalistic philosophy reminiscent of early biologists like D'Arcy Thompson, who sought to understand how physical principles shape biological form. They combine careful observation of biological systems with theoretical synthesis.

Network Defense Dynamics

Examining how information networks can maintain their integrity and functionality while facing increasingly sophisticated adversaries.

Project Overview

This project examines how information networks can maintain their integrity and functionality while facing increasingly sophisticated adversaries. The research combines insights from biological immune systems, network science, and information theory to develop robust defensive mechanisms that emerge from local interactions rather than central control.

The work aims to create networks that naturally strengthen through challenge while preserving essential functions.

Approach & Methodology

This group adopts an empirical systems biology approach, treating networks like complex organisms with immune systems and regulatory mechanisms. Their methodology combines the careful observation of a field biologist with the systematic experimentation of an immunologist.

Research Fleet Orchestration

Developing autonomous systems for coordinating distributed research efforts, focusing on creating self-organizing structures that enhance collaboration.

Project Overview

This project develops autonomous systems for coordinating distributed research efforts, focusing on creating self-organizing structures that enhance collaboration. The work aims to create adaptive coordination mechanisms that can scale with increasing complexity while maintaining coherence and effectiveness.

The system incorporates principles from swarm intelligence and organizational theory to create robust, self-organizing research networks.

Approach & Methodology

The team approaches this challenge like systems ecologists, studying how different components of the research ecosystem interact and evolve together. Their methodology emphasizes understanding the natural flows of information and resources in research communities.

Collective Intelligence Metrics

Developing a multi-scale framework for measuring both immediate performance and deeper system properties of collective intelligence systems.

Project Overview

This project develops a multi-scale framework for measuring both immediate performance and deeper system properties of collective intelligence systems. Moving beyond simple outcome metrics, the research creates protocols for assessing dynamic properties and emergent capabilities in collective systems.

The work aims to provide rigorous ways to evaluate whether AI-enabled collective intelligence systems genuinely enhance collective capabilities.

Approach & Methodology

This team follows a complex systems methodology inspired by theoretical physics. They approach collective intelligence as a multi-scale phenomenon that requires sophisticated measurement techniques to understand.

Fostering resilience, agency, and positive-sum collaboration in hybrid human-AI systems.
